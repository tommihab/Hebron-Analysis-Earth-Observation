# Limitations and uncertainties

Potential issues to evaluate include:
- Cloud/shadow contamination and seasonal comparability
- Differences in source spatial resolution and class definitions
- Mixed pixels and threshold sensitivity
- Orthophoto acquisition conditions and geometric misalignment
- Pretrained model transferability to the local building context
- Limited validation samples and uncertainty of change statistics
- Limits of causal interpretation from optical imagery alone

Document which limitations were measured and which remain untested.
