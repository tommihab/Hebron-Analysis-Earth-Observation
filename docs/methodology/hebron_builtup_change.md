# Methodology — Hebron Built-Up Change Detection (2020–2026)

## Objective

Identify potential built-up expansion and reduction in the Hebron study area by comparing a **2020–2022 baseline** with 2026 using Google Dynamic World built-up probabilities.

## 1. Data and Preprocessing

- **Google Dynamic World V1:** Built-up class probabilities at 10 m resolution.
- **Study area:** Hebron, West Bank.
- **Baseline:** June–September 2020, 2021 and 2022.
- **Comparison:** June–September 2026.

Annual dry-season mean built-up probabilities were calculated for each year. The **2020–2022 baseline** was generated by averaging the three annual composites, with each year weighted equally.

## 2. Built-Up Change Detection

Potential changes were identified by comparing baseline and 2026 built-up probabilities using the following thresholds:

| Parameter | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Expansion | Baseline < 0.20 and 2026 > 0.50 |
| Reduction | Baseline > 0.50 and 2026 < 0.20 |
| Minimum connected pixels | 5 (8-connected) |

Isolated detections were removed using connected-pixel filtering. The resulting change masks, baseline and 2026 probability maps, and probability differences were exported as GeoTIFFs at **10 m resolution (EPSG:32636)** for further analysis and visualisation in ArcGIS Pro.

## 3. Limitations

Dynamic World provides **model-estimated built-up probabilities**, not verified building footprints. Changes may be influenced by bare soil, shadows, mixed pixels, seasonal observation differences, and classification errors.

The selected thresholds may exclude smaller changes or produce false detections. No minimum seasonal observation count was enforced, and the results have not been independently validated.

Therefore, detected changes represent **potential built-up expansion or reduction**, not confirmed construction or demolition.
